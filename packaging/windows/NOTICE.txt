Equipment symbols
-----------------
The canvas symbols are adapted from the ISO 10628-2:2012 symbol sheets by
Steven Baltakatei Sandoval (Baltakatei), 2020, updated 2023:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ISO_10628-2_2012_Symbols.pdf
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Changes: individual symbols extracted as standalone SVG files and embedded in
the program; the pipe lines beside each symbol are omitted, the geometry is
scaled uniformly, curves are flattened to line segments, and the line work is
recoloured. The heating/cooling medium arrow and the feed/product and splitter
symbols are project additions.
