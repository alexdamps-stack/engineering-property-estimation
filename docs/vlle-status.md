# VLLE/LLE stability and flash status (#224)

Status: November 24, 2026. Capability of the PR/PR-BM (cubic) and NRTL-IG
flowsheet packages; GERG-2008 and EOS-CG-2021 return `UnsupportedCapability`.

## Interface

| Layer | Entry points |
|---|---|
| `thermo-solvers` | `vlle_stability`, `vlle_tp_flash`, `vlle_ph_flash` (cubic); `activity_vlle_stability`, `activity_vlle_tp_flash`, `activity_vlle_ph_flash` (gamma–phi); `MultiphaseOptions` |
| `property-packages` | `FlowsheetPackage::vlle_tp` / `vlle_ph` → `MultiphaseEquilibrium` (an `EquilibriumState` plus a `PhaseRole` per phase). Default implementation: `UnsupportedCapability`. |
| `flowsheet-core` | `CalcContext::vlle` / `CalcContext::vlle_ph` |

The existing two-phase `tp`/`ph` operations are unchanged; units that need a
third phase call the VLLE operations explicitly.

## Method

- **Stability.** Tangent-plane-distance trials from a fixed, documented set:
  Wilson vapor- and liquid-like trials (gamma–phi: Raoult K), an *aqueous*
  trial (near-pure water when water is present), a *hydrocarbon* trial (the
  water-free feed over its C/H components, or over all non-water components when
  none is a hydrocarbon) and a near-pure trial for every other component. All
  trials run, so every distinct stationary point is available for initialising
  phases. Passing the trials is evidence, not a proof, of stability; an
  inconclusive trial without proven instability is `NonConvergence`.
- **Split.** Michelsen's multiphase formulation: for fixed fugacity
  coefficients the phase amounts minimise the convex
  Q(β) = Σβ_k − Σ z_i ln Σ_k β_k/φ_ik with β ≥ 0 (projected Newton with an
  active set; a null-space step to the boundary when more phases than components
  are free). Successive substitution with dominant-eigenvalue extrapolation and
  safeguarded Newton steps on ln K converges to max_i spread_k ln(x_ik φ_ik)
  ≤ 1e-10. Vanishing phases are removed; same-kind phases that coincide are
  merged. After convergence the trials are repeated from the equilibrium; a
  negative TPD adds that phase, up to three. A fourth phase is
  `UnsupportedCapability`. The split must lower the Gibbs energy.
- **PH.** Bracketed Illinois regula falsi with a bisection safeguard on h(T) of
  the TP flash, inside the package temperature interval. When the bracket
  shrinks to a few ulp across an enthalpy step (a pure component, or the
  three-phase temperature of a binary at fixed pressure), the phases from both
  sides are combined and their amounts follow from the component and energy
  balances (lever rule).
- **Gamma–phi.** Liquids are NRTL with pure-liquid vapor-pressure standard
  states, the vapor is an ideal gas, as in A7. Each phase keeps the kind it was
  created with; the reference kind of a composition is the one with the lower
  Gibbs energy.

## Labels and outlet mapping

Phases are reported vapor first, then liquids by increasing mass density, with
roles `Vapor`, `LightLiquid`, `HeavyLiquid` (two liquids) or `Liquid` (one).
Cubic: the largest-volume phase of a three-phase split is the vapor; in a
two-phase split it is the vapor unless the single-phase heuristic labels it
liquid (LLE). A single phase follows the single-phase heuristic. Gamma–phi
labels follow the phase kind. Gamma–phi liquid densities come from the
uncoupled saturated-liquid correlation with ideal mixing. Unit outlets map by
role: vapor → vapor, light liquid → light liquid, heavy liquid → heavy liquid;
the unit decides where a single liquid goes.

## Plait points and failures

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| TPD trials too slow to decide (typical just below a plait/critical point) | `NonConvergence`, message names the plait/critical point |
| Split iteration limit with two phases within 0.05 in mole fraction | `NonConvergence`, message names the plait point |
| Phases merge and the stability test keeps re-adding them (more than 4 rounds) | `NumericalFailure`, message names the plait point |
| Converged phases within 0.01 | success with warning `VLLE-NEAR-PLAIT-POINT` |
| Fourth phase indicated | `UnsupportedCapability` |
| PH target outside h(T_min)..h(T_max) | `OutsideValidityDomain` |
| Package without the capability | `UnsupportedCapability` |

Offline tests: `crates/thermo-solvers/tests/vlle.rs` (structure, roles,
aqueous/hydrocarbon trials, permutation invariance, a 200–600 K robustness scan,
PH round trips, the pure-component lever rule against the A5 saturation path,
NRTL LLE activity equality, explicit plait failure, option and domain errors),
`crates/property-packages/tests/vlle.rs` and the `CalcContext` tests in
`flowsheet-core`.

Independent references: `validation/fixtures/vlle-v1.json`, generated by
`validation/julia/generate_vlle.jl` from `validation/vlle-inputs.json` and
compared by `crates/thermo-solvers/tests/vlle_clapeyron.rs`. They are part of the
Clapeyron standard check (`validation/clapeyron-checks.md`).

| Set | Cases |
|---|---|
| PR/PR-BM methane/propane/water and propane/water, explicit water–hydrocarbon kij = 0.5 (approximation) | VLLE at 14, 20 (PR) and 25 bar (PR-BM), LLE at 50 bar, vapor + aqueous liquid at 320 K, dry vapor at 400 K |
| Synthetic NRTL pair (τ12 = 350 K/T, τ21 = 520 K/T, α = 0.2) on the methanol/water pure data | LLE at 300 and 370 K, vapor + liquid at 15 kPa, single liquid, single vapor |
| Physical ChemSep/DECHEMA water/1-butanol NRTL pair (1 atm VLE regression) with ChemSep 1-butanol pure data | LLE at 298.15 and 330 K, a dilute 1 % butanol feed (two liquids: the model solubility is 0.55 %), single vapor at 365 K and 80 kPa |
| PH | round trips of three cubic, two synthetic-NRTL and one water/1-butanol TP state; binary three-phase (invariant) temperature for propane/water at 20 bar, the synthetic pair at 1 bar and water/1-butanol at 1 atm (heteroazeotrope) |

Tolerances (declared before comparison, `atol + rtol·|ref|`): temperature 2e-6 K;
phase fraction 2e-8; composition 1e-8 + 1e-7 rel; molar volume 1e-12 m³/mol +
2e-9 rel; mass density 1e-7 kg/m³ + 2e-9 rel; enthalpy 2e-5 J/mol + 2e-9 rel;
ln φ 2e-9 + 2e-9 rel; Rust fugacity residual ≤ 1e-7.

Clapeyron 0.6.30's native `MultiPhaseTPFlash` is not used alone for phase
counts: with `full_tpd=true` it reports one phase for the 14 bar
methane/propane/water feed, and by default two. The generator therefore solves
each candidate to 256 bits and accepts it only if a composition-grid TPD scan
finds no negative TPD; missing phases are added by splitting a phase with
Clapeyron's two-phase Michelsen flash.

## Model-to-experiment sanity check (water/1-butanol)

Reported separately from implementation agreement. Reference: G. T. Hefter's critical
evaluation in the IUPAC Solubility Data Series, Vol. 15 (Alcohols with Water, 1984),
pp. 33–35: recommended 7.4 ± 0.1 mass % 1-butanol in the aqueous phase and 20.3 ± 0.3
mass % water in the organic phase at 298 K (x = 0.0191 and 0.512). Budgets declared in
`validation/vlle-inputs.json` before comparison: organic x_water within 0.06, aqueous
x_butanol within a factor of 2.

| Quantity at 298.15 K | Model | Experiment | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water in the organic phase, x | 0.601 | 0.512 | +0.089, outside the 0.06 budget |
| 1-butanol in the aqueous phase, x | 0.0055 | 0.0191 | ratio 0.29, outside the factor-2 budget |

The check fails and the budget was not widened. The DECHEMA parameters were regressed
from 1 atm VLE data (373–391 K, an interval inferred from the pure boiling points and
flagged on results) and do not describe the 298 K mutual solubilities: the predicted
liquid–liquid split is qualitatively right (two liquids, organic phase water-rich) but
the aqueous solubility of 1-butanol is under-predicted about 3.4-fold. Units that need
quantitative LLE at ambient temperature need an LLE-regressed parameter set. The
comparison is the ignored test `water_butanol_mutual_solubility_is_close_to_experiment`
(`cargo test -p thermo-solvers --test vlle_clapeyron -- --ignored`).

## Limits

- The interaction parameters of the references are approximations (cubic) or
  synthetic (NRTL): the agreement is implementation verification, not
  experimental accuracy. The physical water/1-butanol pair is VLE-regressed and
  misses the ambient mutual solubilities (see the sanity check above).
- At most three phases. Solid phases and hydrate formation are out of scope.
- The two-phase `tp`/`ph` operations still report a liquid–liquid split as
  unsupported (NRTL) or label it by volume (cubic); units must use `vlle_tp`.
- No PS VLLE flash. Three-phase bubble points (two liquids and an incipient vapor) are
  solved by the cubic bubble/dew operations since #227 ([A13 status](a13-status.md#227-extended-domain));
  there is no three-phase dew point (an incipient liquid is a single phase).
- The desktop interface and `thermo-cli` do not expose the VLLE operations yet.
- Units #176 and #177 remain drawn only; they can now be built on
  `CalcContext::vlle`. The extractor (#189) and the three-phase column (#188)
  use it.
