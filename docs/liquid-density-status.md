# Engineering liquid density (#228) — status

Status: November 27, 2026.

## Decision

The cubic packages (PR, PR-BM, SRK) keep their **untranslated EOS volume**
for every equilibrium, fugacity, caloric and derivative result. Beside it,
they report a separately labelled **engineering liquid density**:

- saturated volume: COSTALD (Hankinson and Thomson, AIChE J. 25 (1979) 653,
  doi:10.1002/aic.690250412) with the Hankinson–Thomson mixing rules;
- water and methanol: the ChemSep v8.3 saturated-liquid correlations
  (DIPPR equation 106 for water, 105 for methanol), because COSTALD misses
  the budgets for them by 24 % and 2–9 %;
- liquids with water or methanol above 0.1 mol %: ideal (Amagat) mixing of
  pure saturated volumes, ChemSep for water and methanol and pure COSTALD
  for the other components. Components without a pure liquid volume at T
  (supercritical, for example dissolved CO₂ or H₂) may total at most
  0.1 mol % and are left out of the sum, which is renormalized;
- compressed liquid: COSTALD-Tait (Thomson, Brobst and Hankinson, AIChE J. 28
  (1982) 671, doi:10.1002/aic.690280420) with the Hankinson–Thomson mixture
  parameters, above the generalized Riedel pseudo saturation pressure of API
  TDB procedure 6A3.4. At or below it the saturated value is reported with
  `pressure_corrected = false`.

A Peneloux-type translation was not chosen. A consistent translation changes
the thermodynamic volume, so it must also correct ln φ (by c·p/RT), enthalpy
and the derivative identities, and it would move every committed PR/PR-BM
reference (Clapeyron, teqp, process fixtures) away from the untranslated
formulation they verify. A separate correlation leaves the equilibrium and
caloric results untouched, as the calculation contract prefers, and is more
accurate for liquid density than a constant translation.

Identifiers: model `COSTALD-HT1979+Tait-TBH1982` v2; data
`costald-hankinson-thomson-1979 + chemsep-8.3-liquid-density`
2026-11-24.1 + 2026-11-27.1.

- `data/source-extracts/liquid-density-costald.provisional.json`: V* and
  ω_SRK from Hankinson and Thomson Table 1, via the MIT-licensed `chemicals`
  1.5.2 (`validation/prepare-costald-source-data.py`).
- `data/source-extracts/liquid-density-chemsep.provisional.json`: ChemSep
  v8.3 (Artistic License 2.0) from the same pinned DWSIM commit as #225,
  written by `validation/prepare-transport-source-data.py`; the notice is in
  `ChemSep-LICENSE.txt`. Hydrogen is excluded: its ChemSep range
  (13.8–33.2 K) lies below every package temperature interval.
- Critical temperatures come from the embedded component database.

## Domain and qualification

| Rule | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 0.25 ≤ T/T_cm ≤ 0.95 and p ≤ 69 MPa | evaluated |
| outside that range | `OutsideValidityDomain`, no extrapolation |
| water or methanol above trace and p > 10 MPa, or T outside the ChemSep range | `OutsideValidityDomain` |
| hydrogen > 0.1 mol % in the liquid | `OutsideValidityDomain` (no qualified correlation) |
| one component above 0.1 mol % (including pure water or methanol), or all such components in {methane, ethane, propane, nitrogen, oxygen, carbon monoxide} | `WithinDeclaredDomain` |
| other mixtures (CO₂ with hydrocarbons, any mixture with water or methanol) | evaluated, `Unassessed` |
| vapor or supercritical phase | not evaluated |
| NRTL-IG, GERG-2008, EOS-CG-2021 | `UnsupportedCapability` |

The 10 MPa limit for water and methanol was set after the comparison,
without changing the budgets: the generalized Tait correction
under-predicts their compression by 2–7 % at 20–50 MPa.

## Evidence (`validation/fixtures/liquid-density-v1.json`)

The fixture is generated by `validation/generate-liquid-density-references.py`
(`validation/liquid-density-requirements.txt`) with no Rust code involved.
Its budgets are registered in `validation/tolerances.json`.

**Implementation verification** against the independent `chemicals` 1.5.2
COSTALD, COSTALD-Tait and DIPPR 105/106 functions covers 270 cases (10 pure
fluids and 10 mixtures; 63 must be refused). The budget is rtol 1e-11 on
molar volume, pseudo saturation pressure, mass density and reduced
temperature.

**Experimental validation** uses 255 points from the CoolProp 7.2.0
reference equations of state, which are correlations of measured densities.
211 of them are in the qualified scope. The budgets were declared before
the first comparison: |ρ/ρ_ref − 1| ≤ 2 % for T/T_c ≤ 0.90 and ≤ 5 % up
to 0.95. All qualified points pass.

| Data set | Method | Max deviation | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| methane, ethane, propane, N₂, O₂ (saturated and compressed) | COSTALD | ≤ 1.03 % | qualified |
| CO₂, CO | COSTALD | ≤ 1.21 % | qualified (pure) |
| 8 hydrocarbon/N₂/O₂/CO bubble-point mixtures | COSTALD | ≤ 0.80 % | qualified |
| water, saturated / compressed to 10 MPa | ChemSep 106 + Tait | 0.15 % / 0.22 % | qualified |
| methanol, saturated / compressed to 10 MPa | ChemSep 105 + Tait | 1.77 % / 1.67 % | qualified |
| propane + CO₂, ethane + CO₂ bubble points | COSTALD | 4.0 % / 9.2 % | unassessed |
| water / methanol above 10 MPa, hydrogen | — | — | refused |

Independence: CoolProp and `chemicals` share no code with the engine. The
critical temperatures from CoolProp 7.2 cubic data are a shared input.

**Identity tests** (`crates/property-packages/tests/liquid_density.rs`):
attaching the density to PR, PR-BM and SRK packages leaves TP, PH and PS
results and ln φ bit-for-bit unchanged. The engineering density is reported
beside the EOS density and differs from it.

## Consumers

- **Stream results:** each liquid `PhaseResult` stores
  `engineering_liquid_density` beside `density_kg_m3` (the EOS density).
  The record holds the density, method and data, validity and pressure
  correction, or the reason it is unavailable. The liquid volumetric flow on
  that density is in `StreamResult::engineering_liquid_volume_flow_m3_s`.
- **Pumps:** `pump_hydraulics` reports the inlet volumetric flow, the
  differential head Δp/(ρ g), the hydraulic power V̇Δp and the NPSH
  available (p_in − p_bubble)/(ρ g). It uses the package bubble pressure at
  the inlet and omits static and velocity head. Each value is *Unavailable*
  with a reason when its input is. Shaft power and the outlet state still
  come from the PS/PH flashes and close the energy balance.
- **Kinetic reactors:** `Kinetics::liquid_volume` selects the liquid molar
  volume used for concentrations and phase volume shares.
  `EquationOfState` is the default and leaves results and fixtures
  unchanged. `EngineeringDensity` fails the unit explicitly where the density
  is unavailable. The choice is saved with the project and omitted when it is
  the default.

Flowsheet evidence: `crates/flowsheet-project/tests/liquid_density.rs` runs
pumps (propane/ethane, water, methanol) and a CSTR through the project
solver, with closed balances and a save/reload round trip.

## Gaps

- Water/methanol mixtures and CO₂–hydrocarbon mixtures are not validated
  (`Unassessed`).
- The water and methanol compressed-liquid domain ends at 10 MPa.
- NPSH available needs the package bubble pressure. For the cubic packages
  that is the bounded A13 domain, so outside it the NPSH is unavailable.
- Relief and pipe sizing (#183, #184), the desktop results panel and the
  reactor editor don't show or set the new fields yet.
