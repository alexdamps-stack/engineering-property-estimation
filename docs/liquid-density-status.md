# Engineering liquid density (#228) — status

Status: November 24, 2026.

## Decision

The cubic packages (PR, PR-BM, SRK) keep their **untranslated EOS volume**
for every equilibrium, fugacity, caloric and derivative result. Beside it,
they report a separately labelled **engineering liquid density**:

- saturated volume: COSTALD (Hankinson and Thomson, AIChE J. 25 (1979) 653,
  doi:10.1002/aic.690250412) with the Hankinson–Thomson mixing rules;
- compressed liquid: COSTALD-Tait (Thomson, Brobst and Hankinson, AIChE J. 28
  (1982) 671, doi:10.1002/aic.690280420) above the generalized Riedel pseudo
  saturation pressure of API TDB procedure 6A3.4; at or below it the
  saturated value is reported with `pressure_corrected = false`.

A Peneloux-type translation was not chosen. A consistent translation changes
the thermodynamic volume, so it must also correct ln φ (by c·p/RT), enthalpy
and the derivative identities, and it would move every committed PR/PR-BM
reference (Clapeyron, teqp, process fixtures) away from the untranslated
formulation they verify. A separate correlation leaves the equilibrium and
caloric results untouched, as the calculation contract prefers, and is more
accurate for liquid density than a constant translation.

Identifiers: model `COSTALD-HT1979+Tait-TBH1982` v1, data
`costald-hankinson-thomson-1979` 2026-11-24.1
(`data/source-extracts/liquid-density-costald.provisional.json`, V* and ω_SRK
from Hankinson and Thomson Table 1 via the MIT-licensed `chemicals` 1.5.2,
URL, SHA-256 and licence retained; critical temperatures from the embedded
component database).

## Domain and qualification

| Rule | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 0.25 ≤ T/T_cm ≤ 0.95 and p ≤ 69 MPa | evaluated |
| outside that range | `OutsideValidityDomain`, no extrapolation |
| water + methanol + hydrogen > 0.1 mol % in the liquid | `OutsideValidityDomain` (not qualified) |
| one component above 0.1 mol %, or all such components in {methane, ethane, propane, nitrogen, oxygen, carbon monoxide} | `WithinDeclaredDomain` |
| other mixtures (CO₂ with hydrocarbons) | evaluated, `Unassessed` |
| vapor or supercritical phase | not evaluated |
| NRTL-IG, GERG-2008, EOS-CG-2021 | `UnsupportedCapability` |

## Evidence (`validation/fixtures/liquid-density-v1.json`)

Generated by `validation/generate-liquid-density-references.py`
(`validation/liquid-density-requirements.txt`); no Rust code is involved.

**Implementation verification** against the independent `chemicals` 1.5.2
COSTALD, mixing-rule and COSTALD-Tait functions: 246 cases (10 pure fluids ×
5 reduced temperatures × 3 pressures, 8 mixtures × 4 × 3; 81 refused), budget
rtol 1e-11 on molar volume, pseudo saturation pressure, mass density and
reduced temperature; refused cases must return `OutsideValidityDomain`.

**Experimental validation** against the CoolProp 7.2.0 reference equations of
state (correlations of measured densities), 229 points of which 160 are in the
qualified scope. Budgets were declared before the

The failing sets were not given wider budgets; the qualified scope was
restricted instead. Reference independence: CoolProp and `chemicals` share no
code with the engine; the engine's critical temperatures come from CoolProp
7.2 cubic data, which is a shared input.

**Identity tests** (`crates/property-packages/tests/liquid_density.rs`):
attaching the density to PR, PR-BM and SRK packages leaves TP, PH and PS
results and ln φ bit-for-bit unchanged; the engineering density is reported
beside, and differs from, the EOS density.

## Consumers

- **Stream results:** each liquid `PhaseResult` stores
  `engineering_liquid_density` (density, method and data, validity, pressure
  correction, or the reason it is unavailable) beside `density_kg_m3` (EOS);
  `StreamResult::engineering_liquid_volume_flow_m3_s` is the liquid volumetric
  flow on that density.
- **Pumps:** `pump_hydraulics` reports inlet volumetric flow, differential
  head Δp/(ρ g) and hydraulic power V̇Δp on the inlet engineering density, or
  *Unavailable* with the reason. Shaft power and the outlet state still come
  from the PS/PH flashes and close the energy balance.
- **Kinetic reactors:** `Kinetics::liquid_volume` selects the liquid molar
  volume for concentrations and phase volume shares: `EquationOfState`
  (default, unchanged results and fixtures) or `EngineeringDensity`, which
  fails the unit explicitly where the density is unavailable. The choice is
  saved with the project (omitted when default).

Flowsheet evidence: `crates/flowsheet-project/tests/liquid_density.rs` (pump
and CSTR through the project solver, closed balances, save/reload).

## Gaps

- Water, methanol and hydrogen have no qualified engineering density; a
  component-specific correlation (for example DIPPR 105 parameters with a
  redistributable source) is needed for aqueous and alcohol liquids.
- NPSH available needs the package's bubble pressure, which arrives with the
  saturation operation of #226.
- Relief and pipe sizing (#183, #184) and the desktop results panel do not
  yet display the new fields; the reactor editor has no control for the
  liquid volume basis (it is set through the project file or API).
